# MedGuard-AI Disclaimer

**OPEN-SOURCE EDUCATIONAL TOOL — NOT FOR CLINICAL USE**

## 1. Not Medical Advice
The MedGuard-AI framework, including its code, data, models, predictions, explanations, and user interface, is strictly an open-source educational tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. 

## 2. No Clinical Decisions
You must NEVER use this tool to make clinical decisions. This tool must not be used to determine whether to start, stop, increase, or decrease any medication.

## 3. Data Limitations
The datasets used in this project are simplified and not comprehensive. The predictions may be inaccurate, incomplete, or entirely incorrect. The patient context features are synthetically generated for demonstration purposes.

## 4. Consultation Required
Always seek the advice of a physician, pharmacist, or other qualified healthcare provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition or medication interaction.
